# exp25 — LEFT-RIGHT axis: orthogonality and salience asymmetry

Three predictions under the negativity-salience hypothesis:
1. cos(A_leftright, A_updown) ≈ 0 — different spatial axis
2. within-pair cos(L_offset, R_offset) > +0.55 (UP/DOWN's) — neither pole has salience-attractor
3. mean LEFT_proj and RIGHT_proj onto A_updown small and balanced — no DOWN-pull

## Interpretation guide

- If within-pair L-R cos > 0.7 AND L/R projections both small (|< 0.5|): negativity-salience hypothesis SUPPORTED
  → LR is a true symmetric spatial axis without the salience asymmetry UD has
- If within-pair L-R cos ≈ 0.55 (like UD): symmetric polar structure, salience hypothesis weaker
- If L and R both project strongly negative (like IN/OUT did): 'any state change' interpretation wins, salience hypothesis weaker
