=== Reading guide ===
  High Spearman (>0.7): orderings agree → cluster structure robust to start choice
  Low Spearman (<0.3):  orderings disagree → greedy procedure unreliable
  Tail stability: axes appearing 4-5/5 times in bottom-4 are robustly independent primitives
  Axes appearing 0-1/5 times in bottom-4 are robustly central (high-connectedness)
